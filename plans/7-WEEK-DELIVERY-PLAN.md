# Seven-Week Delivery Plan

## Week 1 — Foundation and approved homepage

Repository and documentation, application setup, MySQL and Prisma, authentication, permissions, design tokens, navigation, footer, responsive homepage, and homepage approval.

## Week 2 — Admin foundation and catalogue

Admin shell, dashboard, categories, services, media, requirements, SEO, publishing, pricing editor, version history, inventory, and availability.

## Week 3 — Catalogue engine and eligibility

Quests, Diaries, Combat Achievements, Minigames, search, filters, game modes, requirement modal, RSN eligibility, and cart integration.

## Week 4 — Calculators and premium services

Skilling, Bossing, Fire Cape, Infernal Cape, Colosseum, Yama, Royal Titans, Corrupted Gauntlet, Doom, Raids, and server-authoritative pricing.

## Week 5 — Gold, accounts, products, and checkout

Buy and sell Gold, stock and rates, prebuilt accounts, custom builds, Items, Bonds, Outfits, guest checkout, orders, quotes, and payment interfaces.

## Week 6 — Customer dashboard and live chat

Customer dashboard, tracking, progress, Membership, Reviews, custom Socket.IO chat, support dashboard, notifications, retention, and archive.

## Week 7 — Operations, migration, QA, and staging

Reports, audit logs, feature flags, exports, integration status, catalogue migration, content review, full permission and browser QA, staging deployment, fixes, and handover.
